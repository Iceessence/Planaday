Their existence reflects checked-in views/model paths, with verification limits in the [feature matrix](02_COMPLETE_FEATURE_MATRIX.md). Each area is linked to proposals in the [catalog](15_COMPLETE_NEW_FEATURE_CATALOG.md). Related actions should share the same authoritative record and remain available after migration.
